About mandala

A circle of focus

“Mandala” comes from the Sanskrit word for circle or discoid object — a geometric design rich in symbolism in Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

Each guided session is calming, structured and deeply satisfying. You will learn basic mandala patterns and techniques step by step, culminating in a finished dot-art piece of your own.
