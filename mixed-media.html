The workshop

Build, layer, gild

No prior art experience is needed. With step-by-step guidance you will build beautiful textures, add dimension, layer colour and finish with gold-leaf accents — leaving with a one-of-a-kind piece to display.

Included: modeling paste, gold leaf, gold and other acrylic paints, canvas and varnish glaze.
