Founder

Reshu Jain

Born and raised in India, Reshu has always been enthusiastic about art. After school she seemed destined to become a software engineer — until, after her first baby, she realised her mind and soul lay in making art.

Since then she has created vivid, colourful still lifes, landscapes, folk life, nature, abstracts and portraits. Based in the Northern Virginia / DC area with her husband and two children, she teaches beginners and advanced students in her studio — water colour, acrylic, sketching, charcoal, oil pastels and more.

After a hectic day, painting is her most effective form of relaxation. She has taken part in state-level portrait competitions by Art Space, Herndon (VA) and exhibited in Public Art in Reston.
