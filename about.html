About the studio

Two studios, a team of devoted teachers, and one belief — creativity knows no age.

Our story

An artist-led studio

Reshu Art Lobby began as one artist’s wish to share what painting gave her. Today it is a small team of teachers running weekly classes, seasonal workshops, summer camps and celebrations at two studios in Northern Virginia — and online.

Every class is built around patience, encouragement and real technique, for ages four to adult. Beginners are always welcome.
