A studio of many mediums

Learn almost every foundational art form in one place — technique-based, session by session. Ages four and above may join at any point.

01

Watercolours

Brush strokes, landscapes, cityscapes and objects. Learn values, consistencies and colour composition.

02

Acrylic painting

Landscape, still life and abstract on canvas with a palette knife — blending, layering, sponging and texture.

03

Oil pastels

The most-loved medium for young artists: blending, burnishing and layering for landscapes, animals and faces.

04

Pencil sketching

Control, shading and values; drawing human figures, with an introduction to charcoal.

05

Charcoal

The darker side of sketching — realistic portraits built with the grid method.

06

Oil painting

Realism through glazing and layering, from first principles.

07

Mandala art

Geometric shapes and repeating patterns layered into an alluring whole.

08

Marker art

Imaginative colour mixing and shading, explored through themes.

09

Pencil colouring

Soft strokes with Prismacolor pencils for remarkable detail and realism.

010

Egyptian art

Structured, vividly painted forms inspired by the sculpture and culture of Egypt.

011

Clay modeling

Decorative and useful pieces made by hand — including our annual Ganesha idols.

012

Modeling paste

Texture built with paste and tools, dimensional once dry.

013

Gond art

A tribal folk form celebrating the natural beauty around us.

014

Madhubani art

Mithila painting with bold geometric patterns and storytelling.
