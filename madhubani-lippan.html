Madhubani

Mithila painting

Madhubani (Mithila) art originated in the Madhubani district of Bihar, where it was painted on village walls with fingers, twigs, brushes, nib-pens and matchsticks, using natural dyes. It is known for eye-catching geometric patterns that depict mythology, flora, fauna and everyday life.

Taught by fine-art teacher Ms Priya Ranjan, an accomplished watercolour artist whose strengths include Madhubani. Learn birds, animals, nature and expressive human figures, step by step.

Lippan Kaam

Mud & mirror

Step into the traditional beauty of Lippan — the mud-mirror art of Kutch, Gujarat. In this beginner-friendly six-session workshop you will craft a circular piece with clay, mirrors and natural motifs, in colours you choose.

A collaboration with Varnamulu by Sameera. No experience needed.
